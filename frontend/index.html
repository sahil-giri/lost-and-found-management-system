Lost something on campus? Found something?

A simple place for students and staff to report lost items, post found items, and get them back to their owners.

Get started Login

1. Report

Post a lost or found item with its location, date and a photo.

2. Claim

Think it is yours? Submit a claim explaining how you know. The reporter is notified.

3. Return

Once a claim is approved, contact details are shared and the item is marked returned.
